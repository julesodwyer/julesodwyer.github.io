__Jules O'Dwyer__ is Early Career Development Fellow and Lecturer in Film Studies at King's College London. He gained his PhD from the University of Cambridge in 2021, and subsequently held a Research Fellowship at St John’s College, followed by posts in Film Studies at Cambridge and Anglia Ruskin. His research interests include French and Francophone cinema, queer studies, film theory, and spatial approaches to media and thought. He is the author of _The Seduction of Space_ (University of Minnesota Press, 2025) and _Hotels_ (Fordham University Press, 2025), and his work has appeared in journals such as _Screen_, _Discourse_, _Yale French Studies_, and several edited collections. He is co-editor of [_world picture_](http://www.worldpicturejournal.com/).
